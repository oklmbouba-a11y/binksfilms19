# Experimentation Rules — BINKSFILMS LAB

## Modes
### SURGICAL
Minimal blast radius. No architectural redesign.

### EXPERIMENTAL
Baseline first, concept before implementation, QA required.

### RADICAL / BINKSFILMS LAB
Deep restructuring and advanced rendering are allowed. High freedom requires high verification.

## Radical-work protocol
For risky experiments prefer:
1. preserve a known-good checkpoint;
2. prototype in isolation when practical;
3. compare against baseline;
4. validate visual/mobile behavior;
5. measure performance risk;
6. critique originality;
7. integrate only after the new solution earns its complexity.

## Innovation filter
Do not confuse novelty with complexity.
Ask:
- Is this an idea or an effect?
- Why is this specifically BINKSFILMS?
- What can be removed without weakening the concept?
- Is technology louder than the footage/content?
- Does mobile receive an authored equivalent?
