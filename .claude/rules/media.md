# Media Rules

BINKSFILMS is image-led, but media must remain operationally disciplined.

## Preserve these intentions
- defer media until useful;
- avoid unnecessary concurrent video playback;
- keep posters/fallbacks meaningful;
- account for slow networks and Save-Data behavior;
- respect reduced motion where motion is non-essential;
- pause/cleanup media that leaves active context when appropriate;
- avoid forcing high-resolution media when the viewport cannot benefit.

## Creative use
Video may become part of navigation, typography, transition or spatial structure, but loading strategy is part of the design.

## Required for new media systems
Define:
- preload strategy;
- mobile source/crop behavior;
- autoplay policy;
- fallback/poster;
- failure state;
- cleanup/lifecycle;
- reduced-motion behavior;
- network-aware behavior when relevant.
